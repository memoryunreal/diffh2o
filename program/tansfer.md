** Test Metrics **
Based on the research report, the metrics and evaluation criteria for human-object generation focus on both control accuracy and physical plausibility:

Tracking Error Metrics: These evaluate the precision of the generated motions, specifically measuring the Degrees of Freedom (DOF) joint angles as well as the precise 6D positions of the objects.

Task Success Rate: This evaluates the overall effectiveness of the generated policy by measuring how successfully the agent completes the desired interaction or task.


Physical Plausibility and Artifacts: Models are heavily evaluated on their ability to penalize and reduce physical errors that shatter immersion or fail in physics simulators. Key artifacts include:

Spatial Gaps and Floating Contacts: Instances where hands appear to float above a tool or fail to make the required contact.

Mesh Interpenetrations: Errors where the human fingers or body pass directly through the solid geometry of the grasped object.

Foot Skating: Unnatural sliding of the avatar's feet across the floor.
